# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository aims to be a welcoming space for constructive technical discussion and collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and technical decisions—not people.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant, factual, and inclusive.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing or interacting with this project.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that violates this standard through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ReffkiAndreaPratama). Include relevant context and links when possible.

Reports will be reviewed with discretion. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that do not meet this standard.
